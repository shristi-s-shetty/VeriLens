Student Learning Hub

Build your own misinformation radar.

What is Misinformation?

False or inaccurate information spread without the intent to deceive. Still harmful — especially when it goes viral.

What is Disinformation?

Deliberately false information created and shared to mislead, manipulate opinion, or cause harm.

Red Flags in Headlines

ALL CAPS, emotional language, “shocking”, missing sources, or claims that feel too perfect. Pause before sharing.

Check the Source

Who published it? What is their track record? Can you find the same claim on established outlets or primary sources?

Context Matters

Old stories presented as new, cropped videos, or real images with false captions are common manipulation tactics.
